About Capital District Tech Support — Your Trusted Local Tech Experts in Albany, Troy & Schenectady

We're a family-owned tech support business proudly serving the Capital District of New York. Our mission is simple: provide friendly, professional computer help to our neighbors throughout Albany County and beyond.

Our Story

Capital District Tech Support was founded in 2015 right here in Albany, NY. As lifelong Capital District residents, we saw too many neighbors struggling with technology, paying too much for impersonal service from big national chains, or simply going without help.

We started with a simple vision: bring patient, professional tech support to homes and businesses throughout Albany, Troy, Schenectady, and surrounding towns. Ten years later, we're proud to have helped thousands of Capital District residents solve their tech problems.

We're not a franchise. We're not a national chain. We're your neighbors.
